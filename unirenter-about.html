What we do

More than a listings site

Most housing platforms show you listings and leave you to figure out the rest. UniRenter is a settlement companion — from finding your first housemate to managing your tenancy, understanding your rights, and navigating a break lease.

It's built by a student, for students. No advertising, no hidden fees, no spam. Students are free forever.

🎯

Housemate matching

Build a profile in under 5 minutes. We match you with compatible students based on sleep schedules, lifestyle, budget, and location.

🔄

Break lease board

Australia's only free break lease marketplace built for students. Post or find a break lease listing, with a guided six-step handover.

📋

Renter's guide

Plain-English guides covering repairs, bond, inspections, and your rights in each state — always attributing to the relevant authority.

🤠

Cob, your AI companion

Built into every page. Cob nudges you at the right moment — profile tips, date reminders, safety checks on listings, and tenancy questions answered.

🗓️

Tenancy calendar

Connect your lease dates and UniRenter auto-populates your condition report deadline, inspection schedule, renewal window, and notice-to-vacate date.

🇦🇺

Getting settled

Guides for international students settling into Australian life — banking, SIM cards, transport, and finding community in a new city.

Our model

Students pay nothing — ever

UniRenter is free for students, permanently. Revenue comes from the supply side: landlords, agents, and outgoing tenants who need to break a lease pay a small fee. Students never will.

There are no ads on UniRenter. Advertisers don't pay to appear in recommendations, and Cob will never be paid to suggest a product or service.
