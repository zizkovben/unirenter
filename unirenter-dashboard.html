🔑 Before you express interest
Taking over a lease is a legal commitment. Please read and acknowledge the following before continuing.
📋 You inherit the existing lease terms
The rent, end date, and conditions are fixed — you're taking over the current agreement as-is. Read the lease carefully before committing.
✍️ Landlord must approve in writing
The landlord cannot unreasonably refuse, but they must give written consent before the transfer is legally complete. A verbal agreement is not enough.
💰 Bond is transferred separately
Bond is handled between you, the outgoing tenant, and your state authority (e.g. RTBA in VIC, NSW Fair Trading in NSW). UniRenter does not hold or transfer bond money.
📅 You're responsible from handover day
Once the transfer is signed off, rent liability passes to you. Don't move in or pay rent until all paperwork is formally complete.
