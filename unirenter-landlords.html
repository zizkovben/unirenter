Questions

Is listing free? ▾
Waitlist members will get free early access at launch. After the free period, there will be a small listing fee — we'll survey waitlist members before setting prices, so joining the waitlist gives you a say.
Who are the students? ▾
Domestic and international university students across Australia. Every student is OTP-verified by email and completes a detailed housing profile including budget, preferred suburbs, lifestyle preferences and move-in timeline.
Does UniRenter charge a commission? ▾
No. UniRenter is not a real estate agent and charges no commission or percentage of rent. The platform charges a flat listing access fee — not a percentage of any transaction.
Is UniRenter a real estate agent? ▾
No. UniRenter is a lead generation and matching platform only. We hold no licence under the Property and Stock Agents Act 2002 (NSW), Estate Agents Act 1980 (VIC), or Property Occupations Act 2014 (QLD). You manage your tenancy directly.
Which cities are supported? ▾
Student matching is live in Melbourne, Sydney and Brisbane. Adelaide, Perth and Canberra are launching next. Sign up with your city and we'll notify you when your city opens for listings.
What happens to my data? ▾
Your details are stored securely in UniRenter's Supabase database (Sydney region). They are used only to notify you and to inform pricing research. We will never sell your data. You can request deletion at any time — email hello@unirenter.com.au.
